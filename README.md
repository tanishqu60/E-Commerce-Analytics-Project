# 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes an **E-Commerce transactional dataset containing over 10,000+ records** to uncover actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer Segmentation
- Product Segmentation
- Revenue Analysis
- Customer Retention
- Sales Performance
- Funnel Analysis
- Growth Analysis
- Category Performance
- Customer Behaviour Analysis

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs and quickly identify opportunities to improve sales, customer retention, and business performance.

# 📌 Key Insights

### 📈 Revenue Insights

- Electronics generated the highest overall revenue.
- Quarterly revenue peaked during **Q2**.
- Monthly revenue remained relatively consistent across both years.
- Revenue increased by **18% YoY**, indicating healthy business growth.
- Year 2025 (11 months) showed lower overall growth compared to 2024 (12 months).

---

### 👥 Customer Insights

- Repeat customers contributed approximately **65% of total revenue**, highlighting the importance of customer loyalty.
- Total customer base exceeded **20K customers**.
- Customer retention rate was approximately **1.12%**, indicating significant room for improvement.
- Customer growth fluctuated considerably throughout the year.

---

### 🛍 Product Insights

- Electronics emerged as the highest revenue-generating category.
- Category-wise analysis also showed **Pet Supplies** followed by **Toys** as strong revenue contributors during specific analyses.
- High-performing brands consistently generated more customer purchases.

---

### 📊 Funnel Analysis

Customer purchase funnel performance:

| Metric | Result |
|---------|--------|
| View → Cart Rate | **70%** |
| Cart → Wishlist Rate | **78%** |
| Wishlist → Purchase Rate | **59%** |
| Overall Conversion Rate | **32.8%** |
| Drop-Off Rate | **29%** |

The conversion funnel demonstrates strong customer engagement, with a healthy conversion rate and relatively low drop-off.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

## 1. Improve Customer Retention

Current retention rate is approximately **1.12%**, which is significantly low.

### Recommendations

- Introduce loyalty reward programs.
- Offer personalized product recommendations.
- Launch email marketing campaigns.
- Improve post-purchase customer engagement.
- Provide exclusive discounts for repeat customers.

---

## 2. Maintain High Conversion Rate

Current Conversion Rate:

**32.8%**

This is a strong indicator of effective customer acquisition.

### Recommendations

- Continue improving product quality.
- Optimize landing pages.
- Improve checkout experience.
- Promote best-selling products.
- Provide discounts on slow-moving inventory.

---

## 3. Increase Revenue Growth

Although revenue increased YoY, monthly growth remains unstable.

### Recommendations

- Launch seasonal marketing campaigns.
- Improve customer retention.
- Increase repeat purchases.
- Upsell complementary products.
- Cross-sell popular categories.

---

## 4. Focus on High Revenue Categories

High-performing categories include:

- Electronics
- Pet Supplies
- Toys

### Recommendations

- Increase inventory availability.
- Invest more marketing budget.
- Bundle related products.
- Introduce premium product variants.
